## Rules

* While working at your assignment you **cannot consult any teaching
  material** (Moodle page, lecture notes, recordings, etc.)
* You can **look at** and **import** any module from the Agda
  library used in the lectures.
* You can **define any auxiliary function** that may be useful for
  completing the assignment.
* You have **20 minutes** to complete the assignment. When the time
  is over, submit your `.agda` file by sending an email to
  `luca.padovani@unicam.it`.
* Be ready to **explain** any part of the code you submit.

## Assignment

Define a function `sum` that computes the sum of the elements of a
list of natural numbers. Prove that if `xs` is a permutation of
`ys`, then the sum of the elements of `xs` is equal to the sum of
the elements of `ys`.
